The Idea

Rethinking what happens next.

Technology lifecycle management shouldn't end when a device is unplugged.

Retired equipment can still contain sensitive data, reusable components, recoverable materials, and residual value. Without a defined process, those assets can quickly become a security, operational, and waste problem.

Serpex is being built around a simple principle: the end of one technology lifecycle should be the beginning of a responsible next step.

The Process

A structured path from retirement to what comes next.

Know what the asset is, where it goes, what happens to its data, and what happens next.

01

Collect

Receive retired technology through an organized intake and handling process.

02

Secure

Address stored data based on the equipment, customer requirements, and intended disposition.

03

Evaluate

Determine whether equipment should be reused, transitioned, harvested, or recycled.

04

Transition

Move the asset into its appropriate next lifecycle through responsible downstream handling.
